log likelihood

$$
\sum_{\forall(c,t)\in R}\log p(t\vert c,\theta)
$$

将目标函数转换为条件概率

$$
p(t\vert c,\theta)=\frac {e^{s(c,t,\theta)}}{\sum_{\tilde t}{e^{s(c,\tilde t,\theta)}}}
$$

取对数

$$
\log p(t\vert c,\theta) = s(c,t,\theta)-log\sum_{\tilde t}{e^{s(c,\tilde t,\theta)}}
$$
